Safety Instructions

Read and Follow All Instructions

Carefully read through all safety instructions and user guidelines before operating this console.

Water and Moisture

Do not use this console near water, such as near sinks, tubs, pools, or in areas prone to high humidity. Ensure the unit remains dry at all times.

Ventilation

Do not block or cover ventilation openings. Proper airflow is essential to prevent overheating and ensure safe operation. Place the console in a well-ventilated area and avoid placing it on soft surfaces like beds or sofas that could obstruct airflow.

Heat Sources

Keep the console away from heat sources, such as radiators, stoves, or any other device that generates heat. Excessive heat can damage the unit and create a fire hazard.

Power Source

Only connect the console to the power source specified on the unit. If you are unsure of the correct voltage, consult a qualified electrician. Use only the power cord supplied with the device, and never modify or tamper with the electrical plug.

Electrical Safety

Do not defeat the purpose of the grounding (earth) plug by using adapters or cutting off the grounding pin. This plug is a critical safety feature designed to reduce the risk of electric shock.

Lightning and Power Surges

Unplug the console during thunderstorms or when it will be left unused for extended periods. This will help prevent damage due to lightning strikes or power surges.

Cleaning

Unplug the console before cleaning. Use only a dry, soft cloth. Avoid using liquids or sprays that could enter the unit and cause damage.

Service and Repairs

Do not attempt to service or repair the unit yourself. Opening or removing covers may expose you to dangerous voltages or other hazards. Refer all servicing to qualified service personnel.

Accessories and Attachments

Only use accessories or attachments recommended by the manufacturer. Unauthorized modifications or the use of unapproved parts can result in hazards.

Placement and Stability

Ensure the console is placed on a stable surface to prevent it from tipping or falling. Avoid placing heavy objects on top of the unit.

Proper Use

This console is designed for professional audio use only. Do not use it for any other purpose, and ensure all operators are familiar with these safety instructions.

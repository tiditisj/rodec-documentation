General Safety Instructions

To ensure safe and reliable operation of the Rodec ONE, please observe the following guidelines:

Power & Environment

  • Use only the supplied power adapter (12 V DC, center positive).
  • Always switch off and unplug the unit before connecting or disconnecting modules.
  • Do not expose the unit to rain, moisture, or excessive humidity.
  • Keep the Rodec ONE away from direct sunlight, heaters, or other heat sources.

Ventilation & Heat

  • Install the Rodec ONE in a well-ventilated space.
  • Do not cover the unit with cloths or other materials that could block airflow.
  • Allow sufficient clearance around the unit to prevent overheating.
  • The Rodec ONE has been designed with ample stable power, but high-current modules may generate additional heat. Ensure modules have room to dissipate heat.

Stability & Handling

  • Place the Rodec ONE on a stable, flat surface during operation.
  • If mounted in a larger setup or case, ensure the unit is secure and cannot tip or slide.
  • Avoid strong shocks, drops, or placing heavy objects on top of the unit.

Maintenance

  • Do not attempt to service the Rodec ONE yourself.
  • Cleaning should only be done with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use solvents, sprays, or liquids.
  • If the unit is not in use for a long period, disconnect it from the power supply.

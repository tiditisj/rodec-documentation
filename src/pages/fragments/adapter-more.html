03Breakout module

Sidechain, MIDI and CV. In any rack.

A 500 series rack only carries audio and power. The 500 to 100 Series Adapter Breakout Module adds the rest: a single-width 500 series module with CV 1, CV 2, sidechain and MIDI for two adapters, one column of jacks per adapter.

That is what the 100 series modules need for the sidechain and MIDI sync of the SideStepper, and CV control of the M-104 and the MiniStyler.

Requires the adapter MKII.

In the MX Modular you don't need it: the console gives its slots sidechain, MIDI and one CV, from the CV IN and sidechain inputs on the backpanel.

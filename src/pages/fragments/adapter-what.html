01What it does

Balanced outside, unbalanced inside.

Audio

From balanced to unbalanced, and back.

A 500 series rack works with balanced audio, 100 series modules work unbalanced. The adapter de-balances the signal coming in from the rack, sends it through your modules, and balances it again on the way out.

Power

Powered by the rack.

The adapter also converts the power of the 500 series rack for the 100 series modules, so they need nothing else.

Space

One stereo slot, 22HP.

Fill it the way you like: for example 6 + 6 + 10HP (M-108, M-102, M-104), or 6 + 16HP (M-108 and SideStepper or MiniStyler).

Signal chain

Modules in series.

Inside, the modules are linked one after the other with ribbon cables, just like in the Rodec ONE: from the first module to the last, and back to the adapter.
